Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Drift app ("the App") developed by Joel Baum ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the App and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Drift does not require you to create an account and does not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or location.

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymous usage data. This includes information such as how you interact with the game, which features you use, and general device information (device type, operating system version). This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally. You can learn more about how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/privacy.

In-App Purchases

Drift offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited mode. Purchases are processed securely by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage purchase state. RevenueCat may collect a random anonymous user identifier to track whether a purchase has been made. No payment information is ever accessible to us. You can learn more about RevenueCat's privacy practices at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Data Stored on Your Device

Drift stores your game statistics, daily puzzle progress, and purchase state locally on your device using standard browser storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — anonymous usage analytics
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management
  • Google Play — app distribution and payment processing

Children's Privacy

Drift is a word game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The App does not contain advertising, social features, or any mechanism for collecting personal data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
